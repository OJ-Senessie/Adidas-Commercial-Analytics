# Adidas Commercial Performance Analytics

## Overview

An end-to-end commercial analytics project analysing Adidas sales performance across products, retailers, regions and sales channels.

The project uses PostgreSQL and Power BI to identify commercial trends, profitability differences and opportunities for sustainable growth.

## Business Question

How can Adidas understand its commercial performance and convert post-pandemic recovery into sustainable, profitable growth?

## Tools

- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel

## Analysis

The analysis covers:

- Revenue and operating profit
- Operating margin
- Product category performance
- Retailer performance
- Regional performance
- Sales channel performance
- 2020–2021 growth trends
- Product and channel profitability

## Dashboard

The Power BI report contains four sections:

1. Executive Overview
2. Product & Segment Performance
3. Retailer & Geographic Performance
4. Commercial Drivers & Opportunities

## Key Insights

- £899.9M total recorded sales at a 36.9% operating margin.
- 2021 recorded a substantial recovery in sales and profitability compared with 2020.
- The West generated the highest regional sales but had the lowest regional operating margin.
- Online recorded the highest operating margin among the sales methods analysed.

## Recommendations

- Convert recovery-driven demand into repeat purchasing and customer retention.
- Prioritise profitable product and channel combinations.
- Investigate margin performance in high-revenue, lower-margin markets.
- Shift focus from exceptional volume growth toward sustainable, profitable growth.

## Data

The project uses a publicly available Adidas sales dataset covering 2020–2021.

> This is an independent analysis using public/third-party data and is not affiliated with or based on Adidas internal company data.
