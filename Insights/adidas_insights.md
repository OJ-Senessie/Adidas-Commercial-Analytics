# Adidas Commercial Performance — Insights & Recommendations

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines Adidas commercial performance across 2020–2021, focusing on revenue, operating profit, margin, product categories, regions, retailers and sales channels.

The business generated **$899.9M in revenue**, **$332M in operating profit**, and a **36.91% operating margin**, with **2.48M units sold** at an average price per unit of **$45**.

The strongest headline finding is the substantial 2021 recovery following the disruption in 2020. However, the analysis indicates that management should distinguish recovery-driven growth from sustainable long-term growth.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Strong post-lockdown revenue recovery

2021 revenue increased by **294%** compared with 2020.

This represents a significant recovery, but the analysis cautions against treating the increase as a sustainable growth rate because part of the change reflects the post-2020 rebound.

**Business implication:** Management should focus on converting recovery-driven demand into sustainable customer retention and repeat purchasing.

---

### 2. The West is the largest revenue region but has weaker margins

The West generated approximately **$269.9M in revenue** and had a **33.2% operating margin**.

It was the largest revenue region while also having the weakest margin among the regions highlighted in the dashboard.

**Business implication:** The West should be investigated further to understand whether product mix, sales channel mix or retailer mix is contributing to the lower margin.

---

### 3. Sales channels show different commercial profiles

Revenue is split across three sales methods:

- **In-store:** $356.64M — 39.63%
- **Outlet:** $295.59M — 32.85%
- **Online:** $247.67M — 27.52%

The dashboard also compares revenue and operating margin by sales method, allowing channel performance to be assessed beyond revenue alone.

**Business implication:** Channel decisions should consider profitability and productivity alongside revenue contribution.

---

### 4. Product categories contribute differently to revenue and profit

The dashboard compares **Street Footwear, Athletic Footwear and Apparel** across revenue, operating profit and units sold.

This allows product performance to be assessed using both volume and profitability rather than revenue alone.

**Business implication:** Further analysis should identify which product and channel combinations generate the strongest margins and whether high-volume products are also creating sufficient value.

---

### 5. Retailer performance is concentrated

The dashboard compares revenue and operating profit across six major retailers:

- West Gear
- Foot Locker
- Sports Direct
- Kohl's
- Amazon
- Walmart

West Gear and Foot Locker account for substantial portions of the revenue shown in the retailer analysis.

**Business implication:** Retailer-level performance should be monitored alongside profitability to identify opportunities to improve channel productivity and reduce reliance on individual accounts.

---

## Recommendations

### 1. Convert rebound demand into retention

**Why:** The sharp increase in 2021 revenue reflects a significant post-2020 recovery and should not automatically be extrapolated into future growth.

**Action:** Track repeat purchasing, customer retention, revenue per customer and digital engagement to determine how much of the recovered demand is sustainable.

---

### 2. Shift from volume toward value

**Why:** Revenue and unit growth alone do not show whether commercial performance is improving efficiently.

**Action:** Monitor operating margin, product mix, revenue per customer and channel productivity alongside revenue and units sold.

---

### 3. Investigate the West's margin gap

**Why:** The West is the largest revenue region but has a comparatively weaker operating margin.

**Action:** Break down the West by product category, retailer and sales method to identify the specific drivers of the margin difference.

---

### 4. Build a more resilient growth engine

**Why:** A recovery-driven revenue spike is not a long-term strategy.

**Action:** Use the 2021 performance base to improve retention, product mix and channel efficiency while monitoring profitability as the business normalises.

---

## Further Analysis Questions

The current dashboard identifies several areas that warrant deeper investigation:

1. What explains the West region's lower operating margin?
2. Which product categories generate the strongest operating profit relative to revenue?
3. Which sales methods provide the strongest combination of revenue and margin?
4. Which retailer relationships provide the strongest profitability?
5. How much of the 2021 revenue increase is attributable to volume versus price or product mix?
6. Which product-and-channel combinations should receive greater commercial focus?

These questions would form the next stage of analysis before making more granular commercial decisions.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis shows a business experiencing a strong post-lockdown recovery, with substantial revenue and operating profit across products, regions, retailers and sales channels.

The key analytical consideration is sustainability: **the objective should move from measuring the recovery to understanding which parts of the recovery can be converted into durable, profitable growth.**
